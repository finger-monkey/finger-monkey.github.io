01

鲁棒视觉智能 · 跨模态 ReID 与 AI 安全Robust Visual Intelligence · Cross-Modal ReID & AI Security

以跨模态 ReID 为主要载体，从数据不变性、表征不变性进一步推进至任务不变性，围绕“适应变化—发现脆弱—迁移鲁棒”系统研究极端场景泛化、鲁棒表征、跨模态共享脆弱性与少样本知识迁移。

Using cross-modal person re-identification as a primary testbed, this line moves from data and representation invariance toward task invariance, studying extreme-condition generalization, robust representation, shared cross-modal vulnerabilities, and few-shot knowledge transfer.

Extreme GeneralizationRobust RepresentationVulnerability DiscoveryTransferable Robustness
02

Physics AI · 物理智能建模与科学发现Physics AI · Scientific Modeling & Discovery

在鲁棒建模、跨模态表征与知识迁移的方法论基础上，研究物理约束条件下的可解释建模与科学发现，覆盖神经 PDE、物理信息符号回归、物理一致性 4D 生成，并以 3D 重建为 4D 动态建模提供几何与表征基础。

Building on robust modeling, cross-modal representation, and knowledge transfer, this line studies interpretable modeling and scientific discovery under physical constraints, covering neural PDEs, physics-informed symbolic regression, physics-compliant 4D generation, and 3D reconstruction as a geometric foundation for dynamic 4D modeling.

Neural PDESymbolic RegressionPhysics-Compliant 4DScientific Machine Learning

Research Identity

从结构性瓶颈出发，理解知识如何形成与迁移。From structural bottlenecks to transferable knowledge.

两条研究主线虽然面向视觉智能与物理科学两个不同的问题空间，但共同指向“可迁移知识如何形成与传递”这一核心问题。

Although the two research agendas address different problem spaces in visual intelligence and physical science, both ultimately ask how transferable knowledge is formed and transmitted.
